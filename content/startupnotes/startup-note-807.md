刚刚听到到摩根大通 CEO Jamie Dimon 的一段录音，在录音里，他对员工签署请愿书，要求保留混合工作模式——可以到公司，也可以远程办公，做了非常强烈的表达，比如：

- I've been working seven days a goddamn week since Covid, and I come in, and -- where is everybody else?
- And don't give me this shit that work-from-home-Friday works. I call a lot of people on Fridays, and there's not a goddamn person you can get a hold of.
- You don't have to stay at JP Morgan. For those of you who prefer not to work here, that's perfectly acceptable to me.
- Every area should be looking to be 10% more efficient. If I was running a department with 100 people, I guarantee you, if I want to, I could run it with 90 and more efficient.
- These bureaucracies I need more people, I can't get it done... No, because you're filling out requests that don't need to be done.
- It simply doesn't work. And it doesn't work for creativity.
