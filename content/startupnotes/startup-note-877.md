昨天跟同事聊天，他提到对目前在做的产品的“不一样”有点没把握。

我说，有时候，产品理念也是一种不一样，甚至可能是同行不太好模仿的不一样，例如我经常说的简单、长期。

我举例 https://posthaven.com/ 官网上对自己的描述：
- 简单的博客工具
- 承诺不融资、不被收购、不关站
- 永远在线

这几个说法，说起来容易，但真要做到，并不容易。而且还蛮打动我的。

再举一个例子是 Wordpress 的 100 years plan：https://wordpress.com/100-year/，用户也问了，100 年，你们公司都可能不在了，你怎么保障我？他们的回答是：

While our intent is to be around for the next century and host your site, we realize that it is not possible to guarantee this. Therefore, we will keep all 100-Year product accounts in a specially designated internal trust account system. This system may change over the years, but the primary goals are to:

1) Make sure these accounts are flagged internally (to Automattic Inc.) as 100-Year Accounts.

2) Have Automattic Inc (or one of its subsidiaries such as WordPress.com) be listed as an account owner, administrator, billing, or admin contact in order to maintain the product in the event the customer is not responding.
